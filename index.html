The Narrative

It was never simply a romance story.

It is the story of a boy who imagined an entire future with someone. He built a world in his mind—marriage, a home, quiet morning teas, shared dreams, and a family.

Then she left.

But the real tragedy wasn't the loss of a person. The true antagonist of this story is memory itself. The real loss was the architecture of the future he had built around her, now standing empty.
